☾ NACHTLANDER

The 1969 BASIC Lunar Lander.

Relive the first computer game ever written. NachtLander is a faithful recreation of the 1969 Storer/Ahl BASIC lunar lander — the game that started it all — rendered through the warm glow of a vintage phosphor terminal. Choose your fuel burn each turn, manage your descent, and try to touch down softly on the Moon, Mars, Europa, Titan, Ceres, and beyond.

FEATURES

  • Faithful recreation of the original 1969 BASIC lunar lander physics
  • Multiple celestial bodies: Moon, Mars, Europa, Ganymede, Titan, Ceres, Enceladus, Mercury, and Phobos
  • Real-time oscilloscope altitude/velocity display with burn markers
  • Segmented LED-style instrument readouts
  • Synthesised audio engine — all sound generated on-device, no samples
  • Authentic retro CRT display with scanlines, glow, and vignette
  • Green phosphor and amber colour themes
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads
